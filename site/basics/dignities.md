# dignities

## ruler
rul  

the planet which naturally coincides with  
the sign. other planets look to them for  
advice when in their natural signs  

saturn: aquarius, capricorn  
jupiter: sagittarius, pisces  
mars: scorpio, aries  
sun: leo  
venus: taurus, libra  
mercury: virgo, gemini  
moon: cancer  

## exaltation
exa  

planets highest honors  

saturn: libra  
jupiter: cancer  
mars: capricorn  
sun: aries  
venus: pisces  
mercury: virgo  
moon: taurus  

## triplicity
tri  
community support, flowing expression  
through others  

element: day, night, common  
fire: sun, jupiter, saturn  
earth: venus, moon, mars  
air: saturn, mercury, jupiter  
water: venus, mars, moon  

## bound
bou  

family bonds  
small bastions  

## decan
dec  

equal 10 degree splits into three  
home away from home  
minor arcana  

sign: 0-9, 10-19, 20-29  

ari: mars, sun, venus  
tau: mercury, moon, saturn  
gem: jupiter, mars, sun  
can: venus, mercury, moon  
leo: saturn, jupiter, mars  
vir: sun, venus, mercury  
lib: moon, saturn, jupiter  
sco: mars, sun, venus  
sag: mercury, moon, saturn  
cap: jupiter, mars, sun  
aqu: venus, mercury, moon  
pis: saturn, jupiter, mars  

## detriment

opposite their ruling sign.   
general obstacles in the way,   
building in strength  

saturn: leo, cancer  
jupiter: gemini, virgo  
mars: taurus, libra  
sun: aquarius  
venus: scorpio, aries  
mercury: pisces, sagittarius  
moon: capricorn  

## fall
fal  

opposite their exaltation  
being stopped, heavy  

saturn: aries  
jupiter: capricorn  
mars: cancer  
sun: libra  
venus: virgo  
mercury: pisces  
moon: scorpio
