there is no longer any need to animate by a single increment to parse dates, it is now incredibly simple to move forwards or backwards. simply type the increment during chart animation, the current increment is shown in the top right status bar next to the choosen ammount. no need to click any boxes or fiddle with menus! it allows for increments from 0 to 999. typing larger resets back to 1. animating the chart allows for selecting a new increment. give it a try! quickly jump by synodic cycles, or hundreds of years instantly. 

further i have included the addition of LMT (local mean time) for every single IANA timezone, fixing charts drawing incorrectly previously. (such as albert einstein, who was born when LMT was still in effect in his area).

there is also a simple profection calculation from the ascendent added to named charts. It shows the current yearly and monthly sign.
