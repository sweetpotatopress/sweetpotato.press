i can not deny how easily influenced i am by people i think are cool. leah has been harping on ai a lot recently and she makes some good points
i think i still remain neutral. not everyone needs an intimate relationship with the code they are writing. its important to find the boundaries and be mindful of them
the have been a few excellent uses i can not deny. 

for example having it write me a script that ran zdump, parsed the output, and put it into a struct for 550 items. that was really damn cool. i was going to do it the tedious way one by one but it let me do it all at once
using it to transcribe some old astrology lectures into pdf. that was extremely awesome too.
overall though it has been enabling bad habits for me. i could make half working code, give it to ai, and they make it work. but then i miss the process of making it work myself and learn literally nothing, or worse, learn to write broken code
there is a bit of a mystical quality to it, though. letting go of ownership and implementing what is scried in the crystal ball. letting go of chasing something. letting the water reflect  what already is.
