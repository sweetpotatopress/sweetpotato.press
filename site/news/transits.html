huge changes to astro

[link]

astro now has transits! you can play with it by pressing ‘t’ on any chart. it will open an animation menu with the current planets position on the outside of the wheel. the codebase has also been massively overhauled, i removed the ugly new_chart macro and greatly simplified things by adding a struct for ui elements.
though i must admit as of this writing the transit implementation is fresh and could be greatly optimized. please do send bug reports if anything doesnt act as you would expect! thanks!
