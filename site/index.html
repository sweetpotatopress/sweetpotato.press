home of free astrology software astro
available on codeberg

astro is simple, terminal based, astrology software for linux and bsd systems including chart animation, solar returns, saving/loading charts, realtime updates, up to 10 concurrent charts, and more. the data is easily accessible, front and center, for fast reading and delineations, in a compact form factor to easily work with desktop environment windows or window manager scratchpads.
